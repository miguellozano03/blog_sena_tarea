Critical Thinking and Media Literacy

Don’t Believe Everything You Read

Every day we see headlines, videos and posts that compete for our attention. Not all of them are true. Media literacy means asking questions before we accept or share something.

Before sharing, I ask myself:

  • Who wrote this, and why?
  • What evidence supports the claim?
  • Do other reliable sources say the same?
  • Is the headline trying to make me angry or afraid?

Thinking critically is not about distrusting everything. It is about reading carefully and forming my own opinion.

Crime and Punishment

Is Punishment Enough?

When someone breaks the law, society usually responds with a punishment: a fine, community service or prison. But what is the goal? Some people say punishment protects society, others believe it should help criminals change.

Two points of view:

  • Punishment: strong sentences discourage crime and give justice to victims.
  • Rehabilitation: education and job training can reduce the chance of reoffending.

In my opinion, a fair system needs both: consequences for actions and real opportunities to start again.
